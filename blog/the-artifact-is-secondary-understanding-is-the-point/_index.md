# The Artifact Is Secondary, Understanding Is the Point
<time datetime="2026-07-30">Jul, 30 2026</time>

This blog is going to be a little bit personal/emotional. To all job recruiters
looking for a new hire as I'm applying and putting this website on my resume,
this is me. Raw. I dont give a fuck and neither should you.

I regret turning my hobby of programming/computers/whatever into a career
choice. I regret going to school for software engineering and I certainly
regret putting so many hours into making an operating system from scratch using
a fucking hard copy book because no one gives a fuck anymore.

People only care about what you ship and society (as cliche as it sounds) moves
on without you if you don't have a cool product or a big name attached to you.

Going into the industry of software engineering literally ruined my life. I put
so many things on hold in the name of getting "better" at what I do and its all
worthless. AIs read my job applications, not having a profile picture on
linkedin because I dont want randos to look up my name and see a fucking picture
of me apparently makes me less likely to get hired, and human contact is a myth.

I've been searching for a job for so fucking long now it feels like and I don't
really give a shit anymore. I dont want a job I just want to work on things I
would be proud of. A thing where I'm able to rabbit hole into and fucking get
paid for it?! Thats sick.

But I should have never even considered being a software engineer as a
profession. I started learning and teaching myself things before the age of AI
and now with it, its fucking miserable to "compete" with other people. Because
the people that started computer science or software just for the money now have
an edge over the people that started for the love of the game.

The people that only do this sht for money and a successful life welcome AI and
they dont give a single fuck about the loss of craftsmanship and understanding
from the artifacts they produce.

The people that actually care about their work and the process that went into it
are being left behind.

I'm tired of wanting to work on something and then getting that looming feeling
that I'm stupid and slow for not using AI to speedup my workflow.

Dont get me wrong, I definetely use AI and there are significant benefits to it
but I seriously cant vibecode I cant bring myself to do it and its a fucking
disgrace what my hobby has turned into.

I should've went into neuroscience or something that has nothing to do with
software because this field disgusts me and it moves to fast and is overwhelming
for me to even think about.

I miss my early days. The first all nighter I pulled trying to learn java to
make a shitty 2D game, when I finally understood what a pointer is, and best of
all, searching for a solution or that *click* that happens when you scoure the
internet for so long through docs, stack overflow posts, and random forumns to
understand whatever problem I was facing.

I enjoyed software engineering or programming because it felt like I was
actually challenging myself and understanding things as I went along. Now it
just feels like AI will shove the answer down your throat or spoil the answer.

I even had a friend at uni laugh whenever I mentioned that AI "spoils" the
answer. But thats truly how I feel. Its about the understanding and the journey
to me, it was never about the artifact or software I made.

Thats why I have so many unfinished projects from my early days. Something else
would interest me and I would chase that feeling of wanting to understand or
feel like I knew how things work.

I'm not reading this blog post over so the typos and grammar mistakes will be
present and hopefully thats interpreted as an "artistic" touch to what I'm
trying to say here.

Fuck AI and fuck the software industry.
